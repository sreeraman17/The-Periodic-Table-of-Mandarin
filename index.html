How Words Join Up

kind + thing  The last part is the thing; everything before it says what kind. 学校 xuéxiào = the studying kind of school-place; 火车 huǒchē = the fire kind of vehicle; 鸡蛋 jīdàn = the chicken kind of egg; 红茶 hóngchá = the red kind of tea; 周末 zhōumò = the week\u2019s end. The kind can be a noun, an adjective, a color, a time; the rule never moves. Most words live here.
twins glued  Two parts meaning nearly the same thing, or exact opposites, fused: 学习 xuéxí = learn + practise; 帮助 bāngzhù = help + assist; 买卖 mǎimài = buy + sell; 左右 zuǒyòu = left + right, meaning "approximately".
do + what  An action carrying its usual target: 吃饭 chīfàn = eat + meal; 看书 kànshū = read + book; 上班 shàngbān = go-on + shift.
do + result  An action welded to its outcome, including growing into a new state: 听懂 tīngdǒng = listen + understand; 找到 zhǎodào = search + succeed; 长大 zhǎngdà = grow + big.
do + direction  An action plus where it moves: 上来 shànglái = come up; 回来 huílái = come back; 出来 chūlái = come out. (Your addition to the system.)
person-maker  A tail that creates a person: 人 rén, 者 zhě, 生 shēng, 师 shī, 员 yuán, 家 jiā. 工人 gōngrén = worker; 医生 yīshēng = doctor; 记者 jìzhě = reporter. (Your addition.)
place-maker  A tail that creates a place: 店 diàn, 馆 guǎn, 场 chǎng, 室 shì. 商店 shāngdiàn = shop; 饭馆 fànguǎn = restaurant; 教室 jiàoshì = classroom. (Your addition.)
thing-maker  The little tail 子 zi that certifies an everyday object: 桌子 zhuōzi table, 杯子 bēizi cup, 帽子 màozi hat.
sound loan  Borrowed noise, no inner logic: 咖啡 kāfēi coffee, 巧克力 qiǎokèlì chocolate, 沙发 shāfā sofa. Rare, and you can hear the foreignness.
stands alone  A one-syllable word needing no partner: 来 lái, 去 qù, 水 shuǐ, 好 hǎo.
Note: counting phrases (三本书 sān běn shū) and negations (不会 bú huì) are sentences being built, not words being built, so they live in the grammar book, not this table. And 对-words like 反对 fǎnduì (oppose) and 面对 miànduì (face) are filed under do + what: the action aims at 对.
